Show the week, one next step, and visible growth

Taqsha's program is a four-week loop: a daily prompt, a library to work through, a live session, a deliverable, a self-assessment and a mentor score. It ends in a final presentation and a mentor-signed skill profile. The student home should run that loop for the student instead of handing them a menu of modules.

1. What the dashboard is for

Taqsha is a structured, mentored programme where a student produces real work every week and leaves with evidence of skill (a rubric trail and a signed skill profile). Being solo and online, it is most likely to fail through drift: missed prompts, a skipped session, a late deliverable, then silence. So the student home has two jobs:

  1. Keep the student in the weekly loop. Every visit should answer "what do I do now?" in one glance.
  2. Make improvement visible. Scores, self-assessment gaps and the skill profile are why the programme exists. They belong on the home screen, not buried in a report.

2. What's wrong with a feature-first dashboard

The feature list is organised by module: content library, case study library, daily nudge, live session hub, submission upload, rubric views, badges, chat. Built as tiles, that becomes a grid of fourteen doors. The student then has to work out for themselves which door matters on a Thursday of Week 2. Three symptoms to check against the current build:

  • The same layout on day 1 and on deadline day, even though the right next action is completely different.
  • Libraries showing everything, rather than what this week's milestone needs.
  • Mentor feedback arriving as a score with no comparison to the student's own rating and no trend.

3. Principles

The week is the unit

Everything is framed as "Week 2 of 4 · Define the opportunity". The five-step loop (Learn, Live session, Submit, Self-assess, Mentor feedback) mirrors Steps 1–7 of the user journey doc.

One next action, chosen by state

The hero card changes with day, deadlines, feedback posted and inactivity. It never shows two equally loud CTAs.

Only this week's content

The library is filtered and ordered for the current milestone, with must-reads marked. The full library stays one click away.

Growth you can see

Rubric trend per criterion, plus your rating next to the mentor's. The gap appears only after the mentor scores, so self-assessment stays honest.

Recover without guilt

Inactivity turns into a short catch-up plan. Streaks pause instead of resetting to zero. This is the same signal facilitators see as an at-risk flag.

Cohort as presence, not noise

A pulse in the rail (how many answered or submitted, a few updates) gives a solo student company without competing with the work.

7. Decisions needed from the programme team

These are open in the user journey doc, and each one changes what the dashboard shows:

  • Nudge channel. WhatsApp, email or push? I'd go WhatsApp-first with a reply-to-answer link, plus the inline field on home.
  • Late submissions. Blocked, flagged or timestamped? This decides whether the deadline hero shows "late" or "closed".
  • At-risk rule. The prototype assumes 3 days without activity or a missed live session. Students and facilitators should get the same rule.
  • Gap visibility. Should students see their self-assessment next to the mentor's score? I recommend yes, but only after the mentor's score is locked.
  • Certification trigger. Is the mentor's Week 4 sign-off the certificate, or is there a separate admin step?
